FAO-29 Agronomic Formulas & Ground Truth

1. Reference Evapotranspiration (ET₀) — FAO-56 Hargreaves-Samani ET₀ = 0.0023 × (Ra / λ) × (Tmean + 17.8) × √(Tmax − Tmin)

Where Ra is extraterrestrial radiation at latitude 24.2075° N, and λ = 2.45 MJ/kg.

2. Leaching Requirement (LR) — FAO-29 Ayers & Westcot LR = ECw / (5 × ECe − ECw)

For Date Palm, ECe = 4.0 dS/m. With ECw = 3.5 dS/m: LR = 3.5 / (20.0 − 3.5) = 21.2%.

3. Gross Irrigation Depth & System Efficiency Net Depth = ETc / (1 − LR) | Gross Depth = Net Depth / Ea (85%)
4. Al Ain Soil Classification

Hyperthermic Typic Torripsamments: Coarse desert sand with infiltration rates > 50 mm/hr and minimal organic matter. Drip lines require frequent morning pulses to prevent vertical drain losses while keeping salt mobilized.
